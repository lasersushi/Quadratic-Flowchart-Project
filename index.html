Method

Use factoring!

Method Pros Cons
Factoring Very easy and fast when the equation you are trying to solve can easily be factored. Some equations cannot be factored.
Graphing Good when an approximate visual answer is enough, for example finding out how many solutions exist. Less precise than other methods.
Square roots Simple and quick when the equation is in the form x² = d, or when there is no bx and the equation can easily be made into the form x² = d. Only works when the equation is in the form of x² = d and there is no bx term.
Completing the square It is very fast when you have a leading coefficient of 1 or you can easily make it one. It can sometimes involve complex arithmetic, especially if your b term isn't even.
Quadratic formula The universal fallback — it always works. Can be cumbersome to apply and prone to errors.
